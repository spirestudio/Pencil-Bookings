Common questions

Do my clients need an account?

No. They open your link, pick a service, a day and a time, then enter their name and mobile number. That is all.

Do reminders send automatically?

Not yet. Pencil writes each message for you and opens WhatsApp with it ready, so it comes from your own number and replies land in your normal chats. Sending automatically needs WhatsApp’s paid business service, which is not part of the £7.

Where are my bookings stored?

On Pencil’s servers, tied to your account, so you can sign in from any phone or computer. Clients book from your link on their own phones and you see it straight away.

How much does Pencil cost?

£7 a month after a 14-day free trial, with no contract. No card is taken while Pencil is in early access.

Who is Pencil for?

Solo barbers, nail technicians and dog walkers first, because they book by message every day. If you take appointments by DM, it will probably suit you too.
