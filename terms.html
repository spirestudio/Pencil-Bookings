The service

Pencil gives your business a booking page, a calendar and ready-to-send WhatsApp messages. It is in early access, so it is new, may have bugs and may change. Keep your own note of important appointments.

Who can use it

Pencil is for self-employed people and small businesses, aged 13 or over. By creating an account you agree to these terms and the privacy policy.

Price

14 days free, then £7 a month, with no contract. No card is taken during early access. Before any charge begins we will tell you and ask you to agree. You can cancel any time by emailing us or deleting your account, and you will not be charged for the following month. If we change the price we will tell you first, and you can cancel instead.

  • Do not store sensitive information (such as health details) in booking notes.
