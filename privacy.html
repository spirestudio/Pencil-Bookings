Privacy policy

Plain English. Last updated 12 October 2026.

Who we are

Pencil is a booking app for one-person businesses, run from the UK. Contact for anything in this policy: websitebuilds01@gmail.com.

Who is responsible for what

  • Business owners (barbers, nail technicians, dog walkers and similar): for your own account details, Pencil is the “controller”, meaning we decide how they are used.
  • Clients who book: the business you book with is the “controller” of your booking details and decides how to use them. Pencil only stores them on that business’s behalf (we are the “processor”) and does not use them for anything else.

What we collect

  • Owner account: email address, a scrambled (hashed) password, business name, town, phone number, services, prices, opening hours and the other settings you choose.
  • Bookings and clients: a client’s name and mobile number, the service, date and time, price, any note or answer they give (for example a dog’s name), and the status of the booking. Please do not put health or other sensitive information in notes.
  • Payment records: if you pay Pencil, we record the amount, date and method (for example bank transfer) against your account. We do not store card or bank details. These records are kept for up to 6 years for accounting and tax, even after an account is deleted.
  • Public listing (only if the owner switches it on): the business name, town, type and booking page appear in Pencil search. Nothing else is shown, and it is off by default.
  • Sign-in cookie: one essential cookie that keeps you signed in. It is not used for tracking or advertising, so no cookie banner is needed.
  • Technical data: your IP address and, for bookings, the phone number used, kept for up to 24 hours purely to stop spam and abuse (for example, limiting how many bookings or sign-up attempts can be made).

We do not collect payment or card details. We do not use analytics or advertising trackers.

Why we use it, and our legal reason

  • To run the service you signed up for: show free times, take bookings and let owners manage them. (Legal basis: contract.)
  • To keep Pencil secure and stop abuse and double bookings. (Legal basis: legitimate interests.)

We do not sell data, do not share it for advertising, and do not use it to make automated decisions about you.

WhatsApp reminders

Pencil does not send messages itself. It opens WhatsApp on the owner’s phone with a message ready, and the owner chooses whether to send it from their own number. Owners should only message clients about their booking.

Who can see it

A business owner sees only their own bookings and clients. Nobody else can. Pencil is hosted by Cloudflare, which stores the data for us and may process it outside the UK under standard safeguards. The person running Pencil can technically access the database to keep the service working, and will not look at it except to fix problems or when the law requires.

How long we keep it

  • Owner and booking data: until the owner deletes a booking or their account. Deleting an account removes the business, its bookings and its clients.
  • Payment records (amount, date, method): up to 6 years, as required for accounts and tax.
  • Sign-in sessions: until they expire or you sign out.
  • Anti-abuse records (IP addresses, phone numbers used for bookings): deleted automatically within about 24 hours.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you can ask to see your data, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to its use, or have it moved. Business owners can delete their account in the app. Clients should ask the business they booked with, or email us and we will pass it on or help. We will reply within one month. If you are unhappy, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.

Age and changes

Pencil is for people aged 13 and over. If we change this policy in a meaningful way we will update the date at the top.
